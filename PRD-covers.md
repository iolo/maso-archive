# cover restoration

## INPUT
  - new high quality cover images (`covers/YYMM.jpg`)
  - (ignore) old low quality cover images(`covers/masoYYMM.jpg`)

## OUTPUT
  - generate thumbnails and apply them to the reading room webapp
  - delete old low quality cover images(`covers/masoYYMM.jpg`) if the new high quality cover images are available

## IMPORTANT NOTES
- Do **NOT** commit the donated high quality images
- Do **NOT** use the high quality images directly
  - because the donator may not want their high quality images to be used on afraid of copyright issues.
  - also, the high quality images are too large and will slow down the page load.
